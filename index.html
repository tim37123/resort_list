⚠️ Your week is UT's spring break

  • UT Austin's spring break is Mar 15–20, 2027, the same week as your trip, so expect Texas college crowds.
  • The party zones are the Cancún hotel zone strip and Cabo's Médano Beach (Riu Palace Cabo San Lucas). Costa / Playa Mujeres, Nayarit, Turks & Caicos and Negril skew family.
  • Austin nonstops to Cancún and Cabo sell out early for that week. Book flights soon, or pick a fare you can cancel.

🌀 Jamaica after Hurricane Melissa

  • A Category 5 storm hit western Jamaica in Oct 2025. Hilton Rose Hall (now Dreams Rose Hall) and Hyatt Ziva/Zilara Rose Hall are still closed into 2027. Hilton Rose Hall was removed from this list.
  • Open now: Beaches Negril (since Dec 2025), Moon Palace Jamaica, Grand Palladium Jamaica, and Royalton Blue Waters (reopened Sept 15, 2026).
  • Check the Visit Jamaica reopening list before booking. Jamaica also requires an online C5 entry form.

🛂 Passports: check expiration dates now

  • Routine processing is 4–6 weeks, plus up to 2 weeks each way in the mail. Expedited is 2–3 weeks for an extra $60.
  • Kids under 16 get 5-year passports and must apply in person with both parents (or a notarized consent form).
  • Make sure every passport is valid well past Mar 18. Some islands want 6 months of validity, so confirm the rule for your pick.

🎟️ Club-level upgrades

  • At Dreams (Preferred Club), Royalton (Diamond Club), Grand Palladium (Family Selection) and Majestic (Elegance Club), the upgrade adds the premium spirits, a private pool or beach area, and a lounge.
  • If drink quality matters, price the upgrade too. It can close the gap with the Hard Rock, Hyatt Ziva and Beaches tier.

🏨 Sister-resort access

  • Moon Palace Cancún: guests at The Grand can use 25+ restaurants and the pools across the whole complex.
  • Majestic Elegance: the water park is at sister resort Majestic Mirage next door.
  • Riu Guanacaste: free Splash Water World shared with Riu Palace Costa Rica.
  • Grand Palladium complexes share restaurants across their hotels.

💳 Credits & "kids free" offers

  • Palace Resorts (Moon Palace) runs "kids 17 and under stay free" promos plus $250–$750 resort credits.
  • Those credits carry a 16% fee, can't be cashed out, and the dinner perks are usually for 2 adults only. Value them at about half of face.
  • Beaches includes airport transfers, tips and taxes, so its sticker price looks higher than it really is next to the others.

🤿 Included activities that vary a lot

  • Beaches: daily scuba included for certified divers, kids' Bubblemaker / Seal Team programs, an Xbox teen lounge, a teen nightclub and Sesame Street shows. The character breakfast and tuck-in cost extra.
  • Hard Rock: Roxity kids club, bowling (Punta Cana, Los Cabos), mini golf (Punta Cana).
  • Moon Palace / Planet Hollywood: FlowRider surf simulator. It's included at Moon Palace and extra at Planet Hollywood.
  • Royalton Splash Riviera Cancún: trampoline park and laser tag.

🐋 Great March add-ons nearby

  • Vallarta / Nayarit: humpback whale season in Banderas Bay runs through March. A whale-watching boat is a top kid memory.
  • Los Cabos: whale season too, plus the glass-bottom boat to the Arch.
  • Guanacaste: ziplines, plus hot springs and mud baths at Rincón de la Vieja.
  • Cancún: cenote swims and the Isla Mujeres ferry (Playa Norte is calm and shallow).

🧒 Fine print for kids

  • Swim-up suites are often 18+ (Hyatt Ziva Puerto Vallarta) or 13+ (Hyatt Ziva Los Cabos and Cancún).
  • Big slides have height minimums, e.g. about 4 ft at Hyatt Ziva Cap Cana.
  • The Pacific is cooler in March (Cabo about 70–72°F), so heated pools help: Dreams Bahia Mita, and 4 heated pools at Finest.
  • Ask about connecting rooms or 2-bedroom family suites. One room for 4 fills fast in spring break week.

🌊 Seaweed (sargassum) guide & March 2027 outlookWhich beaches are at risk, and how to protect the trip

The seaweed question

Sargassum is an Atlantic and Caribbean problem. It drifts in on east- and south-facing Caribbean shores. The Pacific coast doesn't get it at all. This is how much risk each area carries in mid-March, before the summer peak:

NonePacific coast: Puerto Vallarta / Riviera Nayarit, Los Cabos, Guanacaste (Costa Rica). Sargassum doesn't reach these beaches. The trade-off is cooler water (around 70–73°F in Cabo, 75°F in Vallarta) and some Cabo beaches too rough to swim.
Very lowAruba: sits south of the seaweed belt. Historically the most reliable Caribbean pick.
LowJamaica north & west coasts (Montego Bay, Falmouth, Lucea, Ocho Rios, Negril) and Grace Bay, Turks & Caicos. March is historically clear in both.
Low–moderateNorth-facing Cancún (Punta Cancún) and Playa / Costa Mujeres. These shores are angled away from the main current and resorts clean them daily, but heavy years still reach them.
ModerateCap Cana, Dominican Republic: reef-protected, but Punta Cana overall has been hit hard in recent record years.
Mod–highBávaro south end / Arena Gorda / Macao (Punta Cana). More exposed and rougher surf.
HighRiviera Maya (Puerto Morelos, Playa del Carmen, Akumal, Tulum) and the east-facing Cancún hotel zone. This is the same exposure you ran into last March.

What we know for March 2027

There's no official forecast yet. The University of South Florida usually publishes its first read on the next season around December or January.

Where things stand: 2025 set a record, and 2026 matched or beat it. The September 2026 USF bulletin shows amounts falling in most regions through November, but still above historical norms. Seasons have been starting earlier, which is why March has gone from "usually clear" to "a gamble" on the Mexican Caribbean.
How to protect the trip:
  • Favor Pacific, Aruba, Jamaica or Turks & Caicos picks. If Cancún, stick to Punta Cancún or Costa / Playa Mujeres.
  • Book a refundable or pay-later rate and look again in January and February.
  • Check the USF monthly outlook and a live map such as howisthesargassum.com 2–3 weeks before you leave.
  • Ask the resort whether it has an offshore seaweed barrier and a daily beach crew.
